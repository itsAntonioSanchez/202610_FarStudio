Crime Sudoku

Progress. Crime Sudoku saves your progress and settings on your device and, on Apple devices, in your own iCloud account so you can continue on another device. We cannot see or access your iCloud data.

Ads. The free version shows ads provided by an advertising partner. To show and measure ads, the partner may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address and information about the ads you see. Ads are only personalized if you allow it; you can change this at any time with the "Personalized ads" setting in Options > General, and on iPhone through Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Gameplay statistics. Crime Sudoku uses Unity Analytics to understand how the game is played and to find and fix problems. It collects a random player ID created by Unity, your platform, the game version, your country, how you interact with the game (for example, levels started and finished and features used) and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance. This data is not linked to your name or email and is not used for advertising. Statistics are only sent if you agree. You can turn them off at any time with the "Share gameplay statistics" setting in Options > General; turning them off also asks Unity to delete the data already collected.

Achievements. On Apple devices, achievements use Game Center. Game Center is run by Apple under your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy; we only send which achievements you unlock and do not receive your Game Center profile details.

Purchases. "Remove ads" is an in-app purchase handled by the App Store or Google Play. We only receive confirmation that the purchase exists, never your payment details. Once ads are removed, no more ad requests are made.

Crime Sudoku does not ask you to create an account. Deleting the game removes the data stored on your device; to delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage storage.
