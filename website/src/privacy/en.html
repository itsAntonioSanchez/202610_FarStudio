Who we are

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not ask you for any personal data. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for a short time to keep the site secure and running.

Game trailers are hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play; from then on, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Our games

Our games are designed to work with as little data as possible. What each game collects, if anything, is listed in its own section below. When you buy or download a game through the App Store, Google Play or Steam, that store processes your account and payment data under its own policy; we do not receive your payment details.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
